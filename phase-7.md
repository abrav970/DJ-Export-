# PHASE 7 — SOURCING AND QUALITY STORY

Build the sourcing and quality section around one commercial idea:

Products sourced from Wayanad, Kerala and prepared for international supply.

Do not portray DJ Export as a plantation-management company.

Do not make unsupported claims about farmer relationships, certifications, testing, traceability or processing.

## Story

SOURCE
↓
SELECT
↓
ASSESS
↓
PREPARE
↓
EXPORT

## Visual treatment

Use real product/detail imagery.

Create cinematic transitions between:

source
→ product
→ close-up
→ quality
→ export preparation

Use:

* masked reveals
* depth
* parallax
* typography animation
* ScrollTrigger

Keep the motion slower than the product sequence.

The user should feel increasing confidence rather than visual spectacle.
