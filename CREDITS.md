## ⚠ These are not DJ Export's own facilities

No image on this site depicts a DJ Export estate, warehouse, shipment, employee
or operation. They are placeholders for the visual direction, chosen so that
nothing implies a capability or facility the business may not have:

- Alt text describes **what the photograph shows**, never "our plantation" or
  "our warehouse".
- No image carries a DJ Export mark, sign, or branded packaging.
- Two candidates were rejected during selection for exactly this reason: sacks
  stencilled "OF RWANDA" / "PROD ETHIOPIA" (wrong origin, and reads as a claim),
  and an aerial of the Port of Baltimore (wrong country for an Indian exporter).

Replace these with genuine DJ Export photography before launch. `SHOTLIST.md`
holds the brief for each slot. Swapping one is a `data/media.ts` edit only.
