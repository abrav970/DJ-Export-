# PHASE 10 — FINAL B2B CTA

Create the final DJ Export CTA.

The user should now understand:

* where the products come from
* what DJ Export supplies
* the three core products
* that the business serves international markets

The CTA should therefore be commercial and direct.

Possible direction:

"Looking for pepper, coffee or cardamom from Kerala?"

CTA:

"Request a Quote"

or

"Discuss Your Requirement"

## Animation

Reduce visual complexity.

Use:

* subtle text reveal
* restrained image movement
* gentle scale
* clean CTA entrance

Do not use a dramatic WebGL effect at the end.

The final impression should be:

premium
credible
confident
international
professional
