# PHASE 6 — PRODUCT INFORMATION SYSTEM

Create/refine the commercial information presentation for Pepper, Coffee and Cardamom.

The design must transition from cinematic storytelling to clear B2B information.

For each product support appropriate information such as:

* origin
* available varieties/grades
* specifications
* packing options
* supply information
* relevant certifications/compliance where actually applicable
* inquiry CTA

Do not invent specifications.

Only use information already present in the project or explicitly provided by me.

## Visual direction

Keep the information section restrained.

Use:

* strong typography
* whitespace
* editorial grids
* subtle reveals
* clean comparison/information layouts

Do not turn this into a dashboard.

## Animation

Use subtle:

* masked image reveal
* text reveal
* stagger
* parallax

Do not use complex WebGL here.

The purpose is readability and buyer confidence.
