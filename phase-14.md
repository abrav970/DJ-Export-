## Audit these dimensions

### 1. Visual hierarchy

Is the user's attention directed correctly?

### 2. Depth

Does the page actually feel spatial?

### 3. Motion

Does movement feel cinematic rather than decorative?

### 4. Scroll choreography

Does scrolling feel like controlling a timeline?

### 5. Transitions

Do sections feel connected?

### 6. Typography

Does typography feel premium and editorial?

### 7. Product presentation

Do pepper, coffee and cardamom feel tangible and premium?

### 8. Brand credibility

Does it feel like an actual international exporter?

### 9. Business clarity

Can a buyer understand what DJ Export sells within seconds?

### 10. Performance

Does the animation remain smooth?

### 11. Mobile

Does the experience remain intentional on mobile?

### 12. Accessibility

Does reduced motion remain usable?

## Be critical.

Identify the 10 weakest parts of the implementation.

For each:

* problem
* why it hurts the experience
* recommended fix
* priority

Do not automatically change everything.

Wait for approval before implementing the fixes.
